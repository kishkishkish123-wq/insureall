What home insurance covers

Your home is likely your largest asset. Our policies protect its structure, contents, and your liability as its owner.

  • Dwelling coverage

    Rebuilds or repairs your home's structure after fire, storm, or other covered perils.

  • Personal property

    Replaces belongings — furniture, electronics, clothing — lost, stolen, or damaged.

  • Liability protection

    Covers legal costs and damages if someone is injured on your property.

  • Additional living expenses

    Pays for temporary housing if your home becomes uninhabitable.

  • Other structures

    Covers detached garages, sheds, and fences on your property.

  • Natural disaster riders

    Optional flood, earthquake, and hurricane coverage where standard policies exclude them.
