What education insurance covers

Part insurance, part savings vehicle — designed specifically around the rising cost of education.

  • Guaranteed maturity benefit

    A lump sum paid out when your child reaches university age, regardless of market performance.

  • Premium waiver on disability

    If you become disabled, future premiums are waived while the policy continues growing.

  • Death benefit protection

    If a parent passes away, the full sum assured is paid immediately to fund education.

  • Staged payouts

    Structured disbursements aligned to school milestones — secondary, undergraduate, and postgraduate.

  • Tax-advantaged growth

    Returns accumulate with preferential tax treatment versus standard savings accounts.

  • Study-abroad rider

    Optional additional coverage for international tuition and living cost inflation.
