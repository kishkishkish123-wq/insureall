Our Story

Built out of frustration with fine print.

InsureAll was founded in 1991 by a small team of actuaries and claims adjusters who had grown tired of watching families lose faith in insurance at the exact moment they needed it most.

The company started with a single product — term life insurance — and one operating principle: every policy document should be written so a sixteen-year-old could understand it. That principle has guided every product we've launched since, from automobile to cyber liability.

Today, InsureAll protects over 2.4 million policyholders across 47 countries, spanning every major category of personal and commercial insurance. We remain privately held, which means we answer to our policyholders rather than quarterly shareholder pressure.
