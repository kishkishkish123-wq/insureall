What health insurance covers

From annual checkups to major surgery, our health plans are built around a single principle: care should never wait on cost.

  • Preventive & wellness care

    Annual physicals, screenings, and vaccinations at zero out-of-pocket cost.

  • Hospitalisation & surgery

    In-patient care, surgical procedures, and post-operative recovery support.

  • Prescription medication

    Tiered formulary coverage across generic, brand, and specialty drugs.

  • Mental health services

    Therapy, psychiatry, and inpatient behavioural health treated at parity with physical care.

  • Dental & vision add-ons

    Optional riders covering routine and major dental work, plus eyewear.

  • Maternity & newborn care

    Full prenatal, delivery, and postnatal coverage for growing families.
