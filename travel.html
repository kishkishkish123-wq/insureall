What travel insurance covers

From a cancelled flight to a medical emergency abroad, our travel plans handle the unexpected so you don't have to.

  • Emergency medical coverage

    Treatment, hospitalisation, and emergency evacuation while travelling internationally.

  • Trip cancellation & interruption

    Reimburses non-refundable costs if your trip is cancelled or cut short for covered reasons.

  • Baggage loss & delay

    Compensation for lost, stolen, or significantly delayed luggage.

  • Flight delay & missed connections

    Covers additional accommodation and transport costs from significant delays.

  • Adventure sports rider

    Optional coverage for skiing, diving, and other higher-risk activities.

  • Rental car damage waiver

    Covers deductibles and damage to rental vehicles during your trip.
